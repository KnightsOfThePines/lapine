Understand your rating

Measure your progress.

A rating estimates playing strength from game results. It helps match players with opponents of similar ability.

Which rating do I enter?

Choose the source of your rating when you sign up. Chess.com, Lichess, US Chess, FIDE, and club ratings belong to different player pools and should not be treated as identical.

Your signup rating is self-reported and labeled with its source. If you don't have a rating, choose Unrated. You can still play and join competitions.

How Elo changes

The basic calculation is: new rating = old rating + K × (actual score − expected score). A win scores 1, a draw 0.5, and a loss 0.

Beating a higher-rated opponent usually gains more points than beating a lower-rated opponent. Losing to a lower-rated opponent usually costs more points.

Club rating policy

Organizers assign starting club ratings. This system uses K = 32 and rounds game changes to whole points, adding points to one player and subtracting the same number from the other. Approved organizers save game results through the Admin page; those results update both club ratings. External ratings remain self-reported and separate. The club must agree on starting ratings and which games count before entering real results.
