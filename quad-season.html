Two races to follow

The club championship runs January–June. The Grand Prix runs in four quarters: Winter (January–March), Spring (April–June), Summer (July–September), and Holiday (October–December).

Only January–June periods award championship points. If players finish the championship tied on points, a playoff decides the title; display order alone never awards it.

Grand Prix points

Game score + games played + club check-ins. A check-in counts once per player per date. The same tie-break sequence applies. A player can win only one Grand Prix each calendar year; earlier winners still earn tie-break points but cannot win another quarter.

Format follows Bend Chess & Go's published chess system. No Bend players or competition data have been copied.
