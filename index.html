Who we are

Better chess. Stronger community.

Knights of the Pines brings players together to learn, compete, and build a stronger chess community. Whether you're learning how the pieces move or preparing for your next tournament, there's a place for you at the board.

We value curiosity, fair play, and helping each other improve. Come to play, ask questions, and meet other players.
