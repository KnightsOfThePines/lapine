At the board

  • Be friendly and make games enjoyable for both players.
  • Play quad games at the club. Use a clock set to 10 minutes per player with a 5-second increment per move (10+5).
  • Make it clear that the board is playing a quad game. Observe touch-move, including touch-capture.
  • Check in when you arrive so your opponents know you're available.
  • Record each game separately: a win is 1 point, a draw ½, and a loss 0.
  • An unplayed game scores 0 for both players. It is not a rated loss and does not count as a played game.
  • Let the organizer know before pairings if you will be inactive. Rosters can be adjusted to keep active players playing.

Breaking a tie

  1. Points scored against the other tied player or players.
  2. Number of games completed.
  3. Points in the previous Grand Prix.

For a circular head-to-head tie, such as A beating B, B beating C, and C beating A, use the previous Grand Prix directly. The standings flag any tie that remains unresolved. The organizer confirms the finishing order and records the decision before finalizing the period.

Changes and corrections

Organizers can correct rosters, pairing colors and rounds. A saved result must be undone before its pairing can change. Rated-game corrections follow rating-history order to preserve later Elo calculations.

Based on Bend Chess & Go's chess competition rules, with account and organizer controls built for Knights of the Pines.
