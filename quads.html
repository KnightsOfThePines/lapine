Play & compete

Four players. Three rounds.

A quad is a round-robin group of four players. Everyone plays each of the other three players once.

How a quad works

  1. Players enter the event. Beginners can enter without a rating.
  2. Organizers group players of similar strength into sets of four.
  3. Each player plays three games, one in each round.
  4. A win earns 1 point, a draw ½ point, and a loss 0 points. The highest score wins the group.

The organizer will confirm the time control, dates, rating rules, and tie-break rules before play.
