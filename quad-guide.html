A ladder over the season

January through June, players are arranged in ranked groups of up to four. The top group is the Championship; the next is the Challenger group. Complete your games throughout the month, rather than in a single evening.

Two games per opponent

Play both colors against every opponent. In a four-player group, that means six games per player and twelve games in total. Choose club nights that work for you and your opponents.

Moving between groups

The winner moves up a group, and the last-place player moves down. The top group's winner stays at the top; the bottom group's last-place player stays at the bottom. Organizers can rebalance when people join or withdraw.

Summer and autumn

July–September is the off-season, and October–December is the pre-season. Each uses a three-month super-quad period. Autumn results supply the previous Grand Prix tie-break for January.

Joining or taking a break

Create an account and tell the organizer you want to enter quads. Regular attendance—at least half of club nights—is recommended. Tell the organizer before pairings if you expect to be away. You may ask to rejoin later; the organizer can adjust the roster.
